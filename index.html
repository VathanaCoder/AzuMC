About AzuMC

AzuMC is a catalog for Minecraft Bedrock mods and add-ons, made by Vathana. Browse, search, and save your favorite mods, add-ons, resource packs, maps, and skins.

Settings

Help

Open the Search tab to find something specific. It checks the mod name, description, category, creator, and version, and ignores capitalization. Category chips narrow the results and work together with whatever you typed.

Use the heart to save a mod. Favorites stay in this browser until you remove them or clear site data. Dark and light themes are saved the same way.

Open a mod for the full write-up, preview art, related mods, and and a button to share it. Downloads will appear when a file is ready.

Escape closes an open preview, or leaves a mod page. On a phone, the bottom bar hides while you scroll down and returns when you scroll up.

Settings

Privacy Policy

AzuMC does not ask you to create an account. There is no profile and no upload form.

Your theme and favorite mod IDs are stored in localStorage on this device. They are not sent to a server.

Links to TikTok, YouTube, and Telegram leave AzuMC. Those sites have their own policies. AzuMC does not run analytics and does not sell personal information, because it does not collect it.
